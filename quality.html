The process

Quality is built in four stages.

01

Incoming inspection

Every lot of steel, magnet and bearing is hardness-tested and dimension-checked before release to production.

02

In-process control

Gear runout and tooth profile are measured at each machining stage against tolerance bands.

03

100% final test

Every unit runs noise, backlash, torque and runout checks on an automated test bench before shipping.

04

Life & reliability

New designs pass accelerated endurance testing and thermal cycling before release.
