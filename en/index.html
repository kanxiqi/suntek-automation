Common questions

The details that help you decide.

What are the sample and production lead times?
Prototype samples in 1–5 pieces ship within 48 hours. Production orders typically take 15–25 days, depending on configuration.
Can you customize shafts, windings and flanges?
Yes. OEM options include custom shafts, flanges, motor windings, cables and private-label packaging.
How do I choose a motor and gearbox combination?
Share your load, speed, duty cycle, available space and positioning needs — an application engineer will help narrow down the frame and ratio.
What are your payment and shipping terms?
We accept T/T, L/C and PayPal, and ship FOB, CIF, DDP or EXW. MOQ is 1 piece for samples and 100 pieces for OEM production.
